      How to play

      • Slide. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or swipe. The penguin keeps sliding until something stops it.

      • Rocks and walls stop you on the square right before them. In a maze you slide straight past side passages.

      • Snow stops you right on top of it.

      • Fish are caught when you slide over them.

      • The igloo is home. Reach it to finish the map.

      Earn 3 stars

      1. ★ Get home
      2. ★ Catch all 3 fish
      3. ★ Do both in the fewest possible moves
